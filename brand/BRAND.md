| Accent | `#E8A317` | highlights, motion, wordmark dot |
| Accent soft | `#5EEAD4` | secondary marks |
| Ink | `#0B1220` | body text |
| Surface | `#F2F7F5` | app chrome bg |

| Info | `#0D9488` | info states |
| Success | `#2D874E` | success |
| Warning | `#E8A317` | warning |
